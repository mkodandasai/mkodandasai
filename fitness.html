Rule #1: Do This Before Day 1

Before You Start: Get Your Blood Test & Make a Calendar!

Most people fail in week 2 because they jump straight into starving themselves without knowing what is happening inside their body or having a visual calendar to tick off every day. Do these two things before Day 1:

🩸
Step A

Get a Full Blood Report First

If your Vitamin D, Vitamin B12, Iron, or Thyroid (TSH) are off, you will feel exhausted at 5:30 AM and your body will stubbornly hold onto weight. Fix your internal engine first so your hard work actually pays off—and schedule a follow-up test every 3 to 4 months to see your health markers improve!

★ Real Talk From My Own Journey

"For 10 Straight Days My Weight Didn't Move a Single Gram..."

I want to be 100% honest with you about the most irritating part of losing weight—because nobody warns you about it, and it makes 90% of people quit.

When I started my journey at 126 kg (dropping down to 118 kg, then 114 kg, and pushing toward my 85–90 kg goal), there was a month where for 10 days straight, my weight did not move at all. Even after that, in between weeks, the scale would randomly get stuck again!

Imagine waking up at 5:30 AM every day, walking on the incline treadmill, lifting weights for 45 minutes, working a full 9-to-7 office job, weighing every gram of uncooked rice and paneer, doing your evening walks... and when you step on the scale on Day 6, Day 8, Day 10, the number is frozen.

It is super irritating! Your mind will scream: "Why am I doing all this hard work if nothing is happening?!"

💡 Here is the secret: Even when the scale is stuck for 10 days, your body IS burning fat every single day! When fat cells empty out, they temporarily fill with water to hold their shape. If you don't quit and keep ticking your calendar for those 10 irritating days, your body suddenly flushes the water overnight (called the "Whoosh Effect") and the scale drops 1.5 to 2 kg all at once!

📉 Why Your Scale Gets Stuck (The "Whoosh Effect")

😤
Days 1 to 10 (The Irritating Plateau) You burn fat daily, but sore muscles & empty fat cells hold water. Scale says: 0 kg lost.
🗓️

4 Visual Nutrition Math

How to Calculate Food Calories & Macros Easily

1. Protein

1g = 4 Calories

Builds & protects muscle. Eat 1.6g – 2g per kg of goal body weight.

Foods: Eggs, Paneer, Whey, Curd, Buttermilk, Chicken, Dal

2. Carbs

1g = 4 Calories

Fuels your workouts & brain focus during your 9-to-7 job.

Foods: Rolled Oats, Uncooked Rice, Veggies, Fruits

3. Healthy Fats

1g = 9 Calories

Supports hormones & vitamin absorption. Measure carefully (high calorie!).

Foods: Pure Ghee, Whole Egg Yolks, Omega-3, Nuts

40g Uncooked Rice

Why You Must Weigh Food Raw / Uncooked

When you cook rice or oats, they absorb water, changing their weight completely. Use a digital kitchen scale to weigh raw rice (40g), raw oats (50g), and raw paneer (100g) before cooking, and log them daily.

6 Weigh-In Protocol

When & How to Check Your Weight (Visual Checklist)

🌅

1. Empty Stomach

First thing in the morning after using the washroom, before drinking water.

🧱

2. Hard Flat Floor

Place the digital scale on flat floor tiles—never on carpets or rugs.

👕

Wear minimal clothing so heavy jeans or shoes don't add fake weight.

📅

4. Trust the Average

If it gets stuck for 10 days, don't panic! Compare bi-weekly trends.
